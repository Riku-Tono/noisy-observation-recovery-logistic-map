CONSOLIDATED LIMITED CONCLUSION
======================================================================

Within this fixed sigma and these 20 fixed seeds:

1. Seed 41014 was not the most broadly disrupted seed. It had the fewest total predicate
   crossings and the fewest D events, while having the most near-threshold non-crossings.

2. j=5941 was a common fragile point: it changed 8->0 in all 20 seeds. It was not specific
   to the wrong-estimate incident.

3. The label changes at j=126, j=3116, and j=4994 occurred only in seed 41014 at this
   sigma. Their bracket effects were different.

4. Other seeds commonly added false bracket candidates while retaining the reference
   candidate. This produced nonuniqueness rather than false unique selection.

5. Seed 41014, ell=2 was the only one of 80 seed-by-ell cells where the reference candidate
   was absent and a non-reference candidate was the sole remaining candidate.

6. These data support a stored endpoint description of a candidate-set substitution in
   one fixed run. They do not establish the dynamical cause, an event probability, a
   general failure mechanism, or the effectiveness of any new guard.
